Nana's Garden Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

In short: we don't run any servers, have no accounts, and don't receive or keep your personal information. The game offers optional ads from Google AdMob and an optional purchase through Google Play. Google collects some data to provide those, as described below.

This policy explains how Nana's Garden (“the game”), made by Netharclone (“we”), handles information. It applies to the Nana's Garden app for Android.

Information we collect

We don't collect any. The game has no sign-in and doesn't send your progress or personal information to us.

Information stored on your device

Your garden, coins, water, levels, achievements and settings are saved in the game's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it. If you use Android's own backup feature, Android may include this data in your Google account backup, which Google handles under its own policy.

Ads

The game offers rewarded ads, which only play if you choose to watch one for a bonus. These are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device details, and how you interact with ads. Where required, including in the UK and European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice from Ad privacy choices in the game's Collection tab.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and read Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional No ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether your account owns the purchase.

Permissions

The game uses internet access for ads and purchases, and Google's advertising ID permission for ads. It does not request your microphone, camera, precise location, contacts, or files.

Children

Nana's Garden is intended for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has shared information through the game, please contact us.

Links

The Collection tab has links to this policy and to email us. These open your browser or email app only when you tap them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
