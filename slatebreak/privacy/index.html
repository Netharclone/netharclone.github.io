Slatebreak Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

In short: Slatebreak does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

This policy explains how Slatebreak (“the game”), made by Netharclone (“we”), handles information. It applies to the Slatebreak app for Android.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. We do not use advertising IDs.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your best score, coins, and sound and vibration settings in the game's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it. If you use Android's own backup feature, Android may include this game data in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

Slatebreak contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social features, no in-app purchases, and no links to other apps or websites inside gameplay. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for touch feedback and plays sound effects. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files.

Children

Slatebreak is suitable for all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
