Nana's Codeword Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Nana's Codeword does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking, and it works fully offline.

This policy explains how Nana's Codeword (“the game”), made by Netharclone (“we”), handles information. It applies to the Nana's Codeword app for Android.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. We do not use advertising IDs. The game does not need an internet connection: puzzles, including the daily challenge, are made on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the game you are playing, your statistics (such as games won and best times), your daily challenge calendar, coins, unlocked levels, and settings in the game's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it. If you use Android's own backup feature, Android may include this game data in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

Nana's Codeword contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social features, and no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought. The settings menu has links to this policy and to email us; these open your browser or email app only when you tap them. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for touch feedback and plays sound effects. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files.

Children

Nana's Codeword is suitable for all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
